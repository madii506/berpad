III · HOW IT WORKS

Four moves. One appetite.

01

Launch

Your coin launches on pump.fun through berpad. You set its dip rule once. It can't be changed later.

02

The pot fills

Part of the coin's creator fee goes into its own pot. You keep the rest.

03

The ber eats

When the price falls past the trigger from its recent high, the pot buys the dip and burns it. Every bite is a public transaction.

04

Hibernation

If the coin goes quiet, it hibernates. The pot keeps filling. When volume comes back, the ber wakes up hungry.

V · FAQ

Questions.

What is berpad?

A launchpad for the ber market. Every coin launched on berpad gets a pot that fills from its creator fees and buys the coin's dips by a rule you set at launch. What it buys, it burns.

Can the dev take the pot?

No. The pot only does one thing: buy the coin when the rule triggers, then burn what it bought. Every bite is a public transaction with a link.

Is the launcher live?

Launches open with $BERPAD. The live board and the rule preview work today.

What is hibernation?

When a coin goes quiet for the time you picked, the ber stops hunting and the pot just fills. When volume comes back, it wakes up with everything it saved.

Where do the live coins come from?

Jupiter's public token data (newest and trending pump.fun launches) and GeckoTerminal's pool data for pump.fun and PumpSwap, read live in your browser. Those coins aren't berpad coins.
